\section{Conclusion}
On three audited English news corpora, document-paired validation reproduces the main TF-IDF finding: source masking, length matching and entity masking together remove 26.2\% of the pooled AUC gap, and entity masking improves transfer beyond matched random deletion, including after length matching. For DistilBERT, entity masking performs worse than matched random deletion. Neither transformer has an established positive pooled benefit of S+L+E over its length-matched random control, despite RoBERTa's large raw gap reduction. Dataset-pair effects vary, and the archived transformer pattern is not uniformly supported by the paired run. In a separate 720-fit Celebrity evaluation, TF-IDF's entity-minus-random mean is positive from all three sources, but transformer effects depend strongly on the training corpus; the original pooled negative DistilBERT finding does not transfer uniformly. Artifact-removal gains should therefore be interpreted through document-paired controls and pair-level reporting, with conclusions restricted to the tested corpora and transformations.
