\section{Future work}
\emph{Immediate extensions:} larger external targets beyond Celebrity, including different construction, languages and years; temporally disjoint splits; an independently collected corpus to replace the constructed WELFake residual; and further transformer architectures. \emph{Methodological extensions:} learned or human-validated source-marker detection in place of regular expressions; separating publisher-type signal from veracity signal; counterfactual rewriting as a causal intervention; and evaluation with human-verified labels and instruction-tuned LLMs.



Future releases should retain per-article predictions and validated story groups to support paired article/story bootstrap intervals alongside seed variation.
