\section*{Appendix: Statistical inference and multiple comparisons}\label{sec:appendix}
We emphasize effect sizes, 95\% $t$-intervals over seeds and seed agreement, because the experimental unit is the seed-level repeated run and the six dataset pairs are not independent. As a sensitivity check, with eight seeds the smallest exact paired sign-flip $p$-value is $2/256\approx0.008$, which cannot survive a Holm correction over the seven factorial terms ($0.05/7\approx0.0071$), and $t$-test $p$-values from seed means (about $10^{-5}$ for the length effect) reflect only stability under resampling within one fixed document universe, not generalization.

For the six matched-control contrasts (S against $R_S$, E against $R_E$, S+E against $R_{SE}$, L against C0, L+E against L+$R_E$, S+L+E against L+$R_{SE}$) the smallest attainable $p$ is below the Holm threshold for a family of six ($0.05/6\approx0.0083$), so adjustment is possible in principle. For TF-IDF the exact sign-flip $p$-values are 0.0078 for five contrasts and 0.0156 for S+L+E against L+$R_{SE}$, so all six have Holm-adjusted $p=0.047$; for DistilBERT and RoBERTa no contrast survives (smallest adjusted $p=0.094$ in both). Because the family was defined after earlier exploratory results had been seen, these adjusted values are descriptive and are not a confirmatory test.
