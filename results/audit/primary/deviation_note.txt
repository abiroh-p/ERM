Deviation note

The preregistered thresholds were a minimum of 3,000 documents per class per dataset after cleaning and trimming, and a minimum minority-class share of 40% in each dataset. The primary+sensitivity trim retained 2,964 real and 3,041 fake McIntire documents, so McIntire fell 36 documents below the 3,000-per-class threshold. The corresponding WELFake trim retained 10,417 fake and 6,573 real documents, with a minority-class share of 38.7%; under the primary-only trim, WELFake retained 10,427 fake and 6,734 real documents, with a 39.2% minority-class share. Thus the observed shortfall was 2,964 versus 3,000 for McIntire and 39.2% versus 40% for WELFake in the primary scenario.

We proceeded because the deviations were small, the affected datasets remained near-balanced, ISOT passed the preregistered rule, and the label-direction audit supported the mappings. The WELFake Reuters check showed label 0 overwhelmingly represented Reuters material (21,266 rows versus 16 for label 1), so WELFake was mapped as label 1 = fake and label 0 = real. The corrected rerun removed the prior mass label-direction conflict; remaining conflicts were limited to a small number of copied or near-duplicate items and representation artifacts. The threshold deviation is retained as a limitation rather than concealed by additional post hoc trimming.
